# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want BitterGit to be a place where people can disagree rigorously while
treating one another with dignity. Participation should be safe regardless of
experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality,
religion, or technical preferences.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, curious, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect boundaries and different levels of experience.
- Give credit and correct the record when you are wrong.
- Protect private information and report security issues privately.
- Help de-escalate conflict and make room for others to contribute.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces, community events, and public spaces
where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a participant when necessary to
protect the community.

For a private report, use the repository's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** form and begin the title with `Conduct report`. Include only
the detail necessary to investigate. Maintainers will limit access, avoid
retaliation, and disclose information only as needed for a fair response.
