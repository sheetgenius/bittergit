# Governance

BitterGit is stewarded by Sheet Genius. The project is open to community
contribution, while maintainers remain responsible for source-custody safety,
release quality, and the project boundary.

## Maintainers

- [@ruemic](https://github.com/ruemic)

Maintainers review changes, manage releases and security reports, and may
delegate ownership of a subsystem to sustained contributors.

## Decisions

Routine decisions happen in issues and pull requests through evidence,
maintainer review, and rough consensus. Security, compatibility, licensing,
trademark, and scope-boundary decisions require explicit maintainer approval.

When consensus is not available, maintainers publish the decision and its
reasoning. A verification gate is preferred when a disagreement can be resolved
with an executable contract.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer status is earned through sustained, reliable contributions;
constructive review; security judgment; and care for contributors. Existing
maintainers invite new maintainers and record the change here.

## Contribution terms

Contributions require Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off. There is no
contributor license agreement at this time. Accepted contributions are
licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
